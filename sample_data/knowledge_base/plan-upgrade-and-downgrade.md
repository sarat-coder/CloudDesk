# Plan Upgrades and Downgrades

## Upgrading Your Plan

Upgrades take effect **immediately**. You are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle, then the full new plan amount at the next renewal.

**To upgrade:**
1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Change Plan**.
2. Select the new plan.
3. Review the prorated charge shown.
4. Confirm and pay.

Your new plan limits (storage, seats, API calls) are available instantly.

## Downgrading Your Plan

Downgrades take effect at the **start of the next billing cycle**. You retain your current plan's features until then.

**Before downgrading, check:**
- **Seats in use**: If you have more active members than the new plan allows, you must remove members first.
- **Storage in use**: If your storage usage exceeds the new plan's limit, uploads will be blocked after the downgrade. Remove files first.
- **Features**: SSO, advanced permissions, and priority support are not available on lower-tier plans.

**To downgrade:**
1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Change Plan**.
2. Select the lower tier.
3. Review what features will be lost.
4. Confirm the downgrade.

## Adding and Removing Seats

**Adding seats:** Takes effect immediately and is prorated.

**Removing seats:** Takes effect at the next billing cycle. You are charged for the full current cycle regardless.

## Annual to Monthly Conversion

Switching from annual to monthly billing takes effect at your annual renewal date. Contact support if you need an early conversion — a prorated refund for unused annual months can be arranged.
