# Billing and Refund Policy

## Billing Cycles

CloudDesk offers monthly and annual billing. Annual plans receive a discount equivalent to two free months.

- **Monthly billing**: Charged on the same date each month.
- **Annual billing**: Charged once per year on the renewal date.

## Seat Management

Seats represent the number of members who can be active in a workspace at one time.

- Adding seats takes effect immediately and is prorated to the end of the current billing cycle.
- Removing seats takes effect at the **start of the next billing cycle**. You are charged for the full current cycle.

## Refund Eligibility

CloudDesk offers refunds in the following cases:

| Scenario | Refund |
|---|---|
| Accidental upgrade (within 7 days, no use) | Full refund |
| Unused seats on downgrade | Prorated credit applied to next invoice |
| Service outage > 1 hour (covered by SLA) | Service credit per SLA agreement |
| Duplicate charge | Full refund |
| Unauthorized charge | Full refund, escalated to billing team |

Refunds are **not** issued for:
- Partial use of a billing period after intentional plan changes.
- Feature requests that are not covered by the current plan.

## How to Request a Refund

1. Submit a support ticket with subject "Refund Request".
2. Include your invoice number, the amount, and the reason for the refund request.
3. A support agent will review your account and process eligible refunds within 5 business days.
4. Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

## Payment Disputes

If you believe a charge is fraudulent or was not authorized, contact support immediately. CloudDesk will:
1. Place a hold on further charges pending investigation.
2. Review the charge within 2 business days.
3. Issue a full refund if the charge was unauthorized.

If you have already filed a dispute with your bank, please inform us so we can coordinate.

## Invoices

Past invoices are available under **Settings → Billing → Invoice History**. Each invoice can be downloaded as a PDF.
