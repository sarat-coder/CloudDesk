# Payment Disputes and Unauthorized Charges

## Identifying Unauthorized Charges

An unauthorized charge is one you did not approve. Common causes:
- A team member upgraded the plan without authorization.
- A billing error (e.g., double charge, wrong amount).
- Actual fraudulent activity on your payment method.

## Immediate Steps

1. **Do not wait — contact CloudDesk support immediately.** Disputes have better outcomes when reported quickly.
2. Submit a ticket with:
   - Your account email
   - The charge date and amount
   - The last 4 digits of the card charged
   - A brief description of why you believe it is unauthorized
3. Mark the ticket as **Urgent** or **Billing Dispute**.

## What CloudDesk Will Do

Upon receiving a dispute:
1. Place a hold on the account to prevent further charges pending investigation.
2. Retrieve the transaction record and match it against your account activity.
3. If the charge was erroneous (double charge, billing system error): issue a full refund within 2 business days.
4. If the charge corresponds to a plan change made by a team member: share the audit trail with you and discuss options.
5. If fraud is suspected: notify our security team and coordinate with you on account protection.

## If You Have Already Filed a Bank Dispute (Chargeback)

Please inform CloudDesk support when you file a bank chargeback. Coordination between our teams is important:
- CloudDesk will not issue a separate refund while a chargeback is in progress (to avoid double credit).
- We will respond to the bank's inquiry with transaction records.
- If the bank rules in your favor, the chargeback is processed. If not, CloudDesk will reconsider based on our investigation.

Filing a chargeback without notifying CloudDesk first may temporarily restrict your account per our terms of service. Contacting us first is always the faster resolution path.

## Escalation

If you do not receive a resolution within 5 business days, reply to your support ticket requesting escalation to the Billing Manager. Enterprise customers have a dedicated billing contact; check your onboarding documentation.

## Prevention: Managing Payment Authorization

To prevent unauthorized plan changes:
1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Permissions**.
2. Enable **Require Admin Approval for Plan Changes**.
3. Only workspace owners will be able to change the plan or payment method.
